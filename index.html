05 / 11
05 Rules
Two rules, one price
Rule 1
Rebalance
Back to 25 percent once a year. Browne's trigger: any asset below 15 or above 35 percent.
Rule 2
Never forecast
No timing, no switching. Sell what grew, buy what fell.
The price
25% stocks
Lags far behind in strong stock years. Built to not lose big, not to win big.
